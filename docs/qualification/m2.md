# M2 candidate freeze and remaining limits

The [freeze record](evidence/m2-freeze.json) fixes the parser and collector
candidate before the 18 holdout update pairs are opened. It binds the exact
source tree, executable and helper hashes, development replay, original-archive
checks, black-box controls, and sanitizer campaign. The holdout is a one-time
check of this candidate; its failures will be reported without tuning the
candidate to them.

The immutable development replay covers 72 of the 90 frozen historical update
pairs. The core completed 21 pairs, up from nine before the paired dot-alias
repair. Only nine pairs have complete comparison truth. There are nine known
reporting misses, all in incomplete reviews: artifact changes for local SDKs
and four changed native packages in VS Code, and manifest/artifact changes for
three Realm dependencies. No approval-relevant miss or fact mismatch was
observed in the assessable comparisons. The other 81 pairs in the full corpus
remain partly or wholly unassessed, so those zeros are not coverage claims.

The collector still stops on ordinary inputs outside the declared profile.
Across the two sides of the development pairs, first incomplete reasons include
31 expanded archives over 64 MiB, 18 non-octal tar numeric fields, 12 special
tar entries, six non-ustar archives, six unsupported sources, and missing
artifact locators. These counts are side observations, not distinct packages
or update pairs. Raising size bounds or interpreting additional tar dialects
would enlarge the trusted parser; this candidate retains those limits and
reports incomplete.

The exact candidate passed 306 independent core evidence controls and 19
independent Git-object PR wrapper controls. Its source tree passed all Rust and
Python tests and clippy. Eight ASan differential targets ran 7,160,284
executions for 60 seconds each with no crash. The harness used independently
generated Python seeds plus the measured dot-alias seed. Fixed boundary and
repeated-work tests cover limits that the 64 KiB mutation cap cannot reach.
Finite fuzzing and these controls do not establish absence of parser faults.

Large raw outputs and receipts are retained outside Git under
`/Volumes/Storage/taugate-qualification/candidate-development-v4`,
`m2-final-controls`, `m2-final-pr-controls`, `fuzz-candidate-v4`, and
`dot-path-five-replay`. The development implementation remained unchanged
through replay. The original v4.1 baseline and the pre-dot-alias replay remain
separate measurements.

## Frozen holdout

The [holdout receipt](evidence/m2-holdout.json) records the one-time replay of
the 18 reserved pairs against the frozen binary and unchanged helpers. Eight
pairs were core-complete; only three had complete comparison truth. Two known
non-approval misses in one incomplete Socket.IO review concern a changed
`jake` manifest and artifact. There was no observed approval-relevant miss or
fact mismatch, with the same unassessed-input limitation as development.

Across all 90 frozen pairs, 29 were core-complete and 12 had complete
comparison truth. The two split summaries account for all pairs without
inventing truth for the remaining 78 comparisons. Eleven known reporting
misses remain in incomplete reviews. This cohort is an early compatibility
measurement, not representative coverage or malware detection evidence.
