# Fourth repository-disjoint npm v3 PR sample

The acquisition repair at `f74978c` was frozen before this sample was selected. The sample contains 30 historical npm v3 update PRs from 30 repositories not used in the preceding product cohort. Selection, original source bytes, archive acquisition, and independent labels were sealed before the unchanged `c140aed` checker was replayed. The local study is retained in ignored `ecosystem_audit/next-cohort-20261110`; the [compact receipt](evidence/npm3-next-cohort-20261110.json) binds its frozen inputs and outputs by SHA-256. Public package lifecycle scripts were not run.

The independent oracle could completely label **21/30** pairs. The other nine contain 543 unknown package-side occurrences: 537 missing evidence locators, two manifest/lock dependency disagreements, two duplicate `main` JSON keys, one manifest-name disagreement, and one manifest-version disagreement. Of the 537 missing locators, 497 lacked canonical SHA512 SRI in two PRs and 40 were repeated records for 20 unique archives above the 64 MiB compressed limit in three PRs. The other four partial pairs had manifest or JSON disagreements. These are occurrences across base and head, not distinct packages. The independent-label ceiling was below the declared 80% all-pair floor before product replay.

Across five rounds, **18/30** reviews completed (60%, below the 80% floor), and **18/21** label-complete reviews completed (85.7%, below the separate 95% floor). All 150 processes exited with valid protocol statuses, with no timeout, fact mismatch, or unexpected allowed outcome. Output bytes were stable for each case across rounds. The checker matched **5,709/5,710** assessable events. The sole miss was a non-lifecycle manifest-hash change in `@fastify/send` in [Johnaverse/chains-api](https://github.com/Johnaverse/chains-api); the case returned incomplete. Median of per-case median process times was 21.26 seconds, the slowest process took 59.61 seconds, and maximum process RSS was 848,838,656 bytes. Three workers ran concurrently; acquisition and independent labeling are excluded from these process timings.

The three label-complete but product-incomplete pairs all contain verified `@fastify/send` archives with one PAX entry for `package/test/fixtures/snow ☃/index.html`. Its metadata includes `mtime`; the PAX header uses the basename `PaxHeader/index.html`, and the 4.1.0 archive omits a PAX `size` record. The current collector and checker reject this profile, including its nonportable Unicode path. The independent tar reader can inspect it. This is a measured archive-profile boundary, not evidence of an unsafe approval. Accepting the metadata alone would still leave the PAX target, optional size, and path-portability checks unresolved, so this frozen run receives no repair credit.

Acquisition accounted for 8,725,587,168 bytes within the 16 GiB cap: 5,237 archive identities were available and 20 exceeded the compressed-file limit. Collector validation accepted 39/60 snapshot sides. The nine oracle-partial pairs and three additional product-incomplete pairs remain in the denominator.
